# 📍 Live Location Sharing Guide

## **How to Share Your Live Location on the Map**

### **🚀 Quick Start**

1. **Open the System**: Go to http://localhost:3000
2. **Navigate to Live Tracking**: Click the "Live Tracking" tab
3. **Share Your Location**: Click "📍 Share My Location" button
4. **Allow Permission**: Grant location access when prompted
5. **View on Map**: See your location as a blue bouncing marker

### **📍 Features**

#### **Location Sharing Controls:**
- **📍 Share My Location**: Start sharing your live location
- **⏹️ Stop Sharing**: Stop location sharing
- **🎯 Center on Me**: Center the map on your current location
- **🚌 Test Shuttle Location**: Test shuttle location simulation

#### **What You'll See:**
- **Blue Bouncing Marker**: Your current location on the map
- **Live Updates**: Location updates automatically
- **Speed Information**: Your current speed in km/h
- **Coordinates**: Exact latitude and longitude
- **Status Indicator**: Shows if location is being shared

### **🔧 How It Works**

#### **1. Location Permission**
- Browser will ask for location permission
- Click "Allow" to enable location sharing
- Works best with HTTPS or localhost

#### **2. Real-time Updates**
- Location updates automatically every few seconds
- Blue marker moves as you move
- Speed and coordinates update in real-time

#### **3. Map Integration**
- Your location appears as a blue bouncing marker
- Click the marker to see detailed information
- Use "Center on Me" to focus the map on your location

### **📱 Mobile Usage**

#### **Best Experience on Mobile:**
- Open the system on your mobile browser
- Grant location permission
- Move around to see real-time updates
- Perfect for tracking while traveling

### **🛠️ Troubleshooting**

#### **Common Issues:**

**"Geolocation is not supported"**
- Use a modern browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
- Ensure you're on HTTPS or localhost

**"Location access denied"**
- Click the location icon in your browser address bar
- Select "Allow" for location access
- Refresh the page and try again

**"Location information unavailable"**
- Check if GPS is enabled on your device
- Try moving to an area with better signal
- Ensure location services are enabled

**Location not updating**
- Check your internet connection
- Refresh the page
- Try stopping and starting location sharing again

### **🔒 Privacy & Security**

#### **Your Location Data:**
- ✅ **Local Only**: Location data stays in your browser
- ✅ **No Storage**: Location is not saved to database
- ✅ **Real-time Only**: No location history stored
- ✅ **You Control**: Start/stop sharing anytime

#### **What's Shared:**
- Your current coordinates (latitude/longitude)
- Your current speed
- Timestamp of last update
- **Nothing else is shared or stored**
